## [PowerScaleDB Web App](https://github.com/XanderAP25/PowerScaleDB)
A Flask-based web application for structuring, storing, and analyzing power-scaling data for fictional characters across multiple verses. PowerScaleDB models attributes such as Attack Potency, Speed, Durability, and Hax using a relational schema.

It replaces fragmented spreadsheets and subjective wikis with a consistent data model that supports ranking, comparison, and future analytics.

## [Demystifying AI: A Guide to LLMs](https://xanderap25.github.io/Study-Site/)
A research-driven independent study completed during my senior year. I explored modern language model architecture, in-context learning, fine-tuning techniques, and AI ethics.  

The project culminated in fine-tuning a model on *Grimm’s Fairy Tales* and comparing its outputs to baseline models using structured text analysis and zero-shot prompting.

## [Aquaculture Simulation](https://github.com/ciswashjeff/aquaculture)
A simulation of a closed-loop aquarium ecosystem.  

The model represents fish growth, plant development, and nitrogen cycling, allowing exploration of conditions required for a balanced, self-sustaining environment.

## D3 Track Analysis
A statistical examination of NCAA Division III men’s track performances from the 2023 season.  

The analysis includes regression modeling to study relationships between event times (100m–200m and 200m–400m) and evaluates variance across regional competitive tiers.
