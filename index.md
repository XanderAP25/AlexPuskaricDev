### [Demystifying AI: A Guide to LLMs](https://xanderap25.github.io/Study-Site/)  
An independent study completed during my final semester, focused on understanding how modern language models behave and how they can be adapted for specific narrative tasks. I explored model architecture, in-context learning, fine-tuning strategies, and ethical considerations in AI.

The project culminated in fine-tuning a model on *Grimm’s Fairy Tales* and comparing its outputs against baseline models using zero-shot prompts and text-analysis techniques. The linked site documents these experiments, evaluates how different prompting and training conditions affect model behavior, and includes an introductory article on AI written for an undergraduate audience.
